Good questions

Before you decide.

How much does a luxury bathroom remodel cost?

Our bathrooms start from $18,000. Your final price depends on the size of the room, the layout and the materials you choose. After a free consultation we give you a clear, written estimate, with no surprises.

Do you only do bathrooms?

Bathrooms are our specialty and the heart of what we do. We also design and build kitchens, custom built-in cabinetry and flooring, often as part of a larger renovation, with the same eye for detail and the same clean, professional process.

Why does waterproofing matter so much?

Water getting behind tile is what ruins most bathrooms. We install complete Schluter® or Wedi® systems, so walls and floors are sealed before a single tile goes up, protecting your home for the long term.

Can you help with design and selections?

Absolutely. Every project is custom-designed. We'll guide you through layout, tile, fixtures, vanities and lighting so the finished room reflects your style and works the way you live.
