Meet Eugeniu

Precision is personal.

Eugeniu has spent more than 17 years remodeling homes. Today LuxBath Charlotte focuses on what he does best: high-end, European-style bathrooms with clean geometry, full tile, integrated lighting and professional waterproofing.

Clients describe a rare combination: the skill to build it and the eye to design it. He thinks ahead, finds ways to make a space work harder, and cares about the details most people never notice, from tile alignment to the membrane behind it.

The same standard carries into kitchens, built-in cabinetry and flooring, along with a clean, protected worksite, clear communication and a timeline you can plan around.
